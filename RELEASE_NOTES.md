### Release Overview
We are pleased to announce the **v1.0.0 initial release** of the **Commercial Sub-Critical Hybrid Fission-Fusion Reactor Paradigm**. This release provides a complete, open-source computational simulation suite that demonstrates how coupling a sub-break-even laser ICF trigger ($Q_{\text{fusion}} = 0.1618$) with a sub-critical FLiBe molten salt blanket ($k_{\text{eff}} = 0.965$) transforms a net energy deficit into a grid-ready commercial power plant producing **$+21.5\text{ MW}_e$** ($Q_{\text{electric}} = 1.43$).

### Key Features & Physical Breakthroughs
* **Net Electrical Output ($+21.5\text{ MW}_e$):** Overcomes driver wall-plug inefficiencies through sub-critical neutron multiplication ($M = 113.8\times$) and $18\%$ DPSSL driver upgrades.
* **3D Realism Allowance:** Integrates a $10\%$ neutron streaming penalty through chamber beam ports while preserving commercial profitability.
* **FLiBe Thermal Damping:** Demonstrates that high volumetric heat capacity ($\rho C_p = 4.62\text{ MJ/m}^3\text{K}$) damps $5\text{ Hz}$ energy pulses to an outlet thermal ripple of $\Delta T \le 0.45^\circ\text{C}$.
* **Structural Mechanical Integrity:** Confirms EUROFER97 first-wall stress remains at $108.9\text{ MPa}$, maintaining a $+31.9\%$ yield margin below ASME fatigue limits.
* **Tritium Self-Sufficiency:** Confirms a 3D net Tritium Breeding Ratio ($\text{TBR} = 1.15$) yielding $+18.75\text{ g/day}$ surplus fuel.
