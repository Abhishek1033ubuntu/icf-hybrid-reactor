## 1. Safety Architecture (IAEA SSR-3 Alignment)

| Safety Criterion | Reactor Design Implementation | Compliance Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prompt Criticality Prevention** | Sub-critical operating point ($k_{\text{eff}} = 0.965$) | Passively safe; $\Delta k = 0.035$ margin. |
| **Decay Heat Removal** | Natural convection in FLiBe salt loop | Passive emergency heat exchanger. |
| **Transient Damping** | High salt thermal capacity ($\rho C_p = 4.62\text{ MJ/m}^3\text{K}$) | Thermal ripple $\Delta T \le 0.45^\circ\text{C}$ per pulse. |

## 2. Safeguards by Design (INFCIRC/153 & INFCIRC/540)

* **Denatured Fuel Cycle:** Fissile breeding is constrained to LEU-equivalent thresholds, preventing proliferation-sensitive separation.
* **Continuous Flow Accounting:** Online fluid KMPs sample FLiBe composition at $524\text{ kg/s}$ flow rates to track $^{233}\text{U}/^{239}\text{Pu}$ build-up in real time.
* **Physical Protection:** Compact $1.5\text{ m}$ core footprint simplifies biological shielding and containment boundary safeguards.
